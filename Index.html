Hello, I am a girl from Abuja learning tech through AI for Her

WHO I AM

I am 17 years old from Abuja, Nigeria, learning technology through the AI for Her program. I am just a young girl who is curious and loves to ask questions. Before this program, I thought tech was only for people who are very smart or have big laptops. But now I am learning that with just my phone and determination, I can also build things on the internet.

CHALLENGES I HAVE OVERCOME

Growing up as a girl here, getting access to technology is not easy. Sometimes there is no light, sometimes there is no money for data, and sometimes people will tell you tech is not for girls. I have seen many girls around me stop school to hawk or do house work. I decided I will not be left behind. I use my small phone to watch tutorials, I stay back after school to practice typing, and I never miss any free training. I am breaking that barrier for myself and my younger sisters.

MY TECH DREAMS

My dream is to use technology to help girls in my community who don't have the chance I have. I want to build simple websites and tools that can teach them how to read better or start a small business from home. I also want to teach what I am learning to at least 10 other girls in my street, because if one girl learns, she will teach another. I want to be the reason a girl in Abuja believes she can be in tech.
